Privacy Policy for Chess

Last updated: 2026

This Chess app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Data Collection

The app does not require sign-up, does not use analytics or advertising services, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, or files.

Online Multiplayer

If you use the "Online" mode, a temporary room code and the current game state (board position and moves) are stored only for the duration of your game session, so the two players' devices can stay in sync. No personal information is attached to this data, and it is not used for any other purpose.

Third Parties

This app does not share any data with third parties, as none is collected.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the update will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach the developer through the Google Play Store listing page for this app.
